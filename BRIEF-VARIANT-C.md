Local “cerca de casa” academy: licensed classroom hero, age strip, sticky mobile CTA. No shop.

- **Logo:** ONLY existing `assets/logo-sidebar.png` and `assets/logo-mobile-banner.png`. Never invent crests or new wordmarks.

- **Colors:** existing cream/navy/burgundy/gold in `styles.css`.

- **Public address** MUST be **Nava** everywhere.
- **Public email** MUST be **info@englishstudy.club** everywhere. **NEVER** publish `coppercloud47@gmail.com`.
- **WhatsApp links:** leave as-is unless they break; do not print phone numbers in new copy blocks.
- No AdSense. No shop / products / Shopify storefront.
